--I have tried using MIN(Time) and group by Userid but this just shows the first record for each user in total and not by each day.

--I would like to convert datetime to just date but mainly i need to display the records that show the userid and date but only once per day.


--... and the solution...

Select
	[USERID] As [USERID],
	Min(Cast([Time] As Date)) As [Time]
From @Original_Table
Group By [USERID], Cast([Time] As Date)
--Please also be aware, that the row order of this Select result-set may be different, depending on each instance of computation, depending on many factors, because ordering is handled by the core SQL engine automatically, unless a specific Order By clause is also used after the Group By clause...
