Learning_0001:
(On the theme of Joy)
+Positive Aspect - It is wise to test and analyse any available opportunities, to make sure that you can reach an optimal equilibrium of workload, as a Catholic, without harming or diminishing the degree of Joy that the Lord has gifted you with at any point in time.
+Negative Aspect - If you simply take on everything that comes to your attention, you will likely be overcome, and either start sinning, or possibly worse yet, not be able to give your all, to those people and things which are of the utmost importance... As a side, the Sorrow which is brought about by carrying a 'Heavy Burden' in life, is actually a very good sign. It promises that: 1. You actually care about something of value; 2. The reward that awaits you, should you most likely triumph, is all the greater, as a result.

Learning_0002:
(On the theme of Repentance)
+Positive Aspect - Confessional Neuroticism is not something to avoid, but rather something to embrace... For at least a while... Until you actually start breaking out into a continual Kingdom Joy.
+Negative Aspect - The ignorance of one's own state of sinfulness, is indeed a dangerous situation to be in... I recommend that if you are still being tempted to sin, then you should go to confession roughly every fortnight, because you have likely not properly confessed every Grave Sin that you have historically committed, or, God forbid, are currently committing... Demons should have no chance in causing you to sin, if there are no open sin wounds in your body or soul... As a side, Perfect Contrition for each and every Personal Sin, is a necessary accomplishment, in order to progress to the positive aspect of this theme of Repentance.
